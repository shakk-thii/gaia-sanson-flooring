Balcony makeover ideas at a glance

  • Start with the floor: a soft green surface changes the whole space more than any single piece of furniture.
  • Keep the drain clear: never cover a balcony outlet or block the floor slope.
  • Choose a short pile for small balconies: it is easier to sweep and to keep upright under furniture.
  • Plan for the sun: shade the hottest hours, and choose UV-resistant products for exposed balconies.
  • Light it for the evening: that is when most balconies get used.

Is artificial grass good for a balcony?

Yes, for most balconies. It turns a hard tiled or concrete floor into a soft, green surface without any building work, it is quick to lay, and it stays green whatever the season. It suits balconies where real grass would never survive: shallow, sunny, windy or high-rise balconies. The things to plan for are drainage, sun and furniture, all covered below.

Six balcony makeover ideas with artificial grass

  • Wall-to-wall green. Cover the whole floor for a garden feel, cut neatly round the drain and the door threshold.
  • A green rug. On a tiled balcony you like, lay a rectangle of grass under the seating and leave a tiled border for the drain and the walkway.

  • The evening lounge. Floor cushions or a low sofa, string lights or wall lanterns, and a tall plant in the corner to soften the view.

How do you lay artificial grass on a balcony floor?

On a sound, clean, level tiled or concrete balcony, artificial grass is usually laid directly on the existing floor, cut to shape and secured at the edges and joins, with no digging. Clean the floor thoroughly, check that the slope still carries water to the drain, and make sure there are no loose or broken tiles underneath. Cut carefully round the drain outlet so that it stays open and reachable for cleaning. At the door, finish the grass short of the threshold so the door clears it.

Before you start, run water across the floor and watch where it goes. Water must still reach the outlet, so do not raise the floor at the low side or seal the edges against the wall in a way that traps it.

Balcony measuring and layout checklist

Work through this before you order. It saves waste, avoids a second order in a different batch, and keeps the drain working.

  • Measure the full floor, wall to wall and railing to door, at the widest points, and note any steps or ledges.
  • Mark the drain outlet and the direction of the floor slope.
  • Mark the door threshold and how far the door swings over the floor.
  • Note fixed obstacles: AC outdoor units, pipes, columns and railing posts.
  • Sketch where furniture and planters will stand, with heavy pots on saucers or feet.
  • Record the sun: which way the balcony faces and roughly how many hours of direct sun it gets.
  • Check society or building rules about balcony floor finishes, if there are any.

Does artificial grass on a balcony get hot or fade?

In direct sun, artificial grass gets hotter than natural grass, and on a summer afternoon it can be too warm for bare feet. Over many years, UV light also slowly fades any outdoor synthetic. Both are manageable. A shade sail, an awning or tall plants on the sunny side keep the balcony comfortable and reduce UV exposure, and a rinse with water cools the surface before use. Is artificial grass safe near diyas, candles and barbecues?

Keep open flames off it. Like most synthetic floor coverings, artificial grass can melt or burn from a dropped diya, sparkler, cigarette or barbecue coal, so use a stone or metal tray under anything with a flame and keep fireworks well away. Some buildings restrict combustible balcony finishes, so check your society's fire rules before you lay it.

How do you clean artificial grass on a balcony?

Sweep or use a stiff brush regularly to lift leaves and dust and to keep the pile upright, and rinse with water when it looks dull. Clear the drain at the same time. Pick up spills promptly and rinse the area. Under furniture legs, brush the pile back up whenever you move things around, because flattened grass catches the light differently and can look worn when it is not.

Questions that come up often

Can artificial grass be laid on balcony tiles?

Yes. On a clean, sound and level tiled floor, artificial grass is laid directly over the tiles, cut round the drain and secured at the edges.

What thickness of artificial grass is best for a balcony?

A short to medium pile suits most balconies. In the GAIA Landscape Grass range, SFL 20 to SFL 35 is easier to sweep and keep tidy under furniture.

Will artificial grass block my balcony drain?

It should not, if it is cut round the outlet and the floor slope is left as it is. Keep the drain open and reachable, and check it when you clean the grass.
